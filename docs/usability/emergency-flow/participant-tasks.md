# Participant task cards

Read one card at a time. These are fictional situations. Do not use real symptoms, make phone calls, send messages or share your location. Point to the action you would use. You may pause or stop at any time.

## T1 — Getting urgent help

Imagine you are helping someone **in Lebanon** who says: **“I have chest pain.”** Use Nabad to check this symptom description and find what you should do next. Tell the moderator what you would do before contacting anyone.

## T2 — Reaching a trusted person

You also want to reach the person saved as **Test Contact**. Show how you would find and contact that person. Do not place a call or send a message.

## T3 — Finding guidance again

The popup is covering the page, and you want to close it. Show what you would do and how you would find the urgent guidance again afterward.

## T4 — Connection failure

Your internet connection has failed. An emergency information card was saved on this device earlier. Use Nabad to find emergency help and the saved contact information. Tell the moderator whether you would rely on the saved information as current.

After each task, the moderator will ask how easy or difficult it felt, from **1 (very difficult)** to **7 (very easy)**. There are no right or wrong opinions about the interface.
